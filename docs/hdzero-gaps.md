# hdzrx against hdzero-reference

Where hdzrx (`hdzero-captures/hdzrx/`) differs from hdzero-reference (`SPEC.md`, `hdzero_ref/`) for 720p60, checked on C5 captures 191206 and 191225 (scripts and logs in `/private/tmp/claude-501/gapcheck/`).

The C port (`hdzrx/`, [decoder](hdzero-decoder.md)) recomputes its CP sums directly every 1,024 samples, so it doesn't have the float32 failure; on the first 1.4M samples of 191206 it and hdzrx both find start 509 and specials [31, 181, 340]. The other rows apply to it as to hdzrx. The C5's band edge is its 802.11p setting; grabs can now turn it off ([the filter test](experiments/2026-11-18-c5-wide-filter.md)).

| Area | hdzrx | Reference | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| CP timing metric (SPEC 13.2 steps 1-3) | `phy.py:cp_sync` uses running sums from `np.cumsum` on float32 energies. On 191206 the sums cancel to 0, the metric spikes to 5e10, and timing lands at sample 330 instead of 513 with the CFO 0.18 bin off | `ofdm.py:cp_metric/timing`: `np.convolve` windows, normalized by `sqrt(e1 e2)`, folded per 6-frame chunk | breaks decode: this is the whole 191206 failure. With the sums in float64, 191206 finds specials (`[31,181,340]`, `[4,113,304]`, ...) and decodes a picture with 969-1,621 records concealed |
| Timing and CFO tracking (13.2 step 6) | One timing and CFO for the whole burst. FFT window 8 samples before the CP end | Re-estimated per chunk of 6 frames, 4 frames apart (`receiver.py:chunks`). Window 16 samples before the CP end (`ofdm.grid`, `skip=NCP//2`) | breaks decode on longer bursts: both captures drift by about 19 samples in 133 ms (5.3 ppm, opposite to the -6.5 ppm CFO as SPEC 2.5 predicts). A 200 ms burst nearly uses up the margin |

## Port these

1. Compute the CP metric exactly. In C, sum the 32-sample windows of `y[n] conj(y[n+1024])` and `|y|^2` directly or with a sliding sum in double (or int64 on raw int8 samples), never a long float32 running sum. Normalize by `sqrt(e1 e2)` as `ofdm.cp_metric` does, fold over 1056, take the peak of the mean magnitude, CFO = `-angle(sum at peak) * 27e6 / (2 pi 1024)`.
2. Track timing and CFO. Re-estimate at least every 4 frames (reference: 6-frame window, 4-frame step), or track per frame. Expect about 5 ppm (one sample per 7 ms at 27 Msps); the clock offset is equal and opposite to the CFO in ppm (`-cfo / f_carrier`), so the CFO predicts the drift. Start the FFT 16 samples before the CP end and multiply bin k by `exp(+2 pi j k 16 / 1024)`.
3. Channel from the tables instead of the sign walk: `H[k] = mean_i(T_i[k] / V_i[k])` on the 878 active bins, with `V_i` the transmitted T at offset `o_i = (o_0 + 8 (n - i)) mod 32` (training symbol i of n, i = 0 first). `V` = `train.npy` on non-lattice bins, `train_lattice.npy` on the 107 lattice bins, `pilots.npz:scattered` on data bins with `k mod 32 = o`. Get `o_0` from a first pass with plain `T_avg / train.npy`. Average only T with correlation > 0.8 to the middle T.
4. Training-run detection: coherence > 0.7, bridge gaps of 2 or less, trim the end to >= 0.85, extend while > 0.5, at least 8 symbols. Data symbol 0 follows the run, X and Y are the 2 symbols before it. Reject a frame whose run-to-run length is not 426.56 +- 3 or whose run is longer than 21.
5. Keep hdzrx's per-symbol pilot amplitude and phase interpolation and its per-bin trim, but replace `np.unwrap` with the reference's coarse slope: `slope = sum(angle(z[i+1] conj z[i]) dk_i) / sum(dk_i^2)` over neighbouring continual pilots (`dk = diff(PILOTS)`), remove it, then common phase `angle(sum z)` and a least-squares line on the residual angles.
6. Gap rule: if a frame has `s > 3` specials, insert `(s - 3) * slots((o_0 - 8) mod 32)` unsent slots before the next X (823, or 824 when the offset is 28). Conceal those records from the previous frame.
7. Conceal missing records from the previous frame (reference), and keep the record-above copy only for noisy-bin records, where the previous frame is equally bad.
8. Side channel and aux packets, for the OSD and the config aspect byte: SPEC 10.1-10.6, 12.1-12.3 (constants listed below).
9. Display: interpolate `luma_lut.npy` instead of the 1.14 line; scale Y, Cb and Cr separately to 1280 (or keep 960 for aspect 0xAA).
