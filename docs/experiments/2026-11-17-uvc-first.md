# 2026-11-17: UVC at full rate while it streams

## Question

Nathan wants UVC's frame rate first, with the LCD taking whatever is left. Which LCD policy keeps UVC at the source's 59.9 fps for a minute with nothing dropped, and gives the LCD the most?

## Setup

- Commit: this one, on 2d1c.
- Bitstream: `lockout_regs_top` in the Nano's flash.
- Boards: the pinned p4vrx P4, C5 and Nano, external antenna, a VTX on R4.
- Wiring changes from `../wiring.md`: none.

## Method

macOS as the UVC host through camgrab. Counter differences over 62.6 s (`/tmp/rate.py`, outputs in `captures/cpu/`): fields decoded, frames encoded, sent, refused and skipped by UVC, LCD frames shown.

- `video lcd auto`, the new default: while a host takes frames, the LCD gets every other field, scaled before the encoder runs.
- `video lcd adaptive`: each field is encoded first, and the LCD takes it if the PPA has finished the last one.

## Results

| Policy | UVC sent/s | Refused, skipped | LCD fps | Encode ms | Core 0 idle | Core 1 idle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| auto (every other field) | 59.9 | 0, 0 | 30.0 | 9.1 | | |
| adaptive | 57.9 | 0, 0 | 57.7 | 17.1 | 40.9% | 36.0% |

- Fields decoded: 59.9/s in both.
- macOS leaves the stream open after ffmpeg exits, so TinyUSB still reports it streaming while nobody reads; every frame is refused. The P4 now counts the host as taking frames only while fewer than 30 in a row have been refused. Meanwhile it encodes one field in 30 to notice a reader coming back, and the LCD returns to every field: 59.8 fps, 2 frames/s refused.
- Two host restarts in a row each picked the stream back up; a 10 s clip from each decoded.

## Verdict

Adopted: `auto`. UVC keeps every field and the LCD runs at 30 fps while a host reads, and 59.8-59.9 fps otherwise. Adaptive keeps the LCD near 58 but costs UVC 2 fps, because the PPA and encoder still overlap. `video lcd N|adaptive|auto` and `video stagger` stay for experiments.

## Next

Nothing for now. If the LCD at 30 fps while streaming bothers anyone, the strip copy and the PPA's picture pass are where the remaining bandwidth goes.
