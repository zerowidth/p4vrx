# 2026-11-16: decoded video on the LCD

## Question

Can the board's 4.3-inch panel show the decoded picture at 60 fps while UVC streams?

## Setup

- Boards: P4 (p4vrx), Nano with `passthru_lockout_top` in flash, C5 on `firmware/c5`, R4.
- Panel: ST7701, 480x800 portrait over two-lane MIPI-DSI at 500 Mbit/s a lane, RGB565, 30 MHz pixel clock (576 x 870 clocks a frame, 59.9 Hz). DSI PHY power from LDO 3 at 2.5 V, backlight PWM on GPIO 26 (active low), reset on GPIO 27, as in Waveshare's BSP. None of these are header GPIOs.

## Method

`lcd.c` brings the panel up with the BSP's init table and `espressif/esp_lcd_st7701`, no LVGL. The video task offers each decoded field (packed 4:2:0, the encoder's O_UYY_E_VYY, which the PPA reads as YUV420) to an LCD task. The task has the PPA scale it to 630x480 (0.875 across, for NTSC's 4:3 on square pixels), turn it a quarter turn and convert it to RGB565, straight into one of three DPI frame buffers, then shows that buffer from the next refresh. With no decoded fields, the panel shows the same status screen UVC gets. `lcd` reports the rates; `lcd rotate` turns the picture half a turn; `lcd backlight N` and `lcd show on|off` are for tests.

## Results

- Two frame buffers, waiting for the refresh after each swap: 49.8 fps, the PPA taking 8.9 ms a frame.
- Three frame buffers: 58.9 fps with UVC idle; 59.8 fps with UVC streaming (1498 of 1499 frames shown, no missed refresh), video at 59.9 fps, the PPA taking 11-12.5 ms.
- CPU with all three running: demodulation and sync on core 1 60.4%, drawing on core 0 46.4%, no overruns or dropped lines.
- Tip noise at 345-351 kHz, the same with the backlight off or on and with the PPA stopped. The AGC sat at gain 70, against 58 earlier at this distance, so the signal was about 12 dB weaker.

## Verdict

Adopted. The panel shows live video at 59.8 fps alongside UVC, with no cost to the decoder.
